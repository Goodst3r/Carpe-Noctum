Calculation rules reviewed

  • Use dB losses and gains at the same frequency and measurement plane. Apply only remaining test-point coupling once.
  • Cable attenuation interpolation, temperature correction and passive path losses use the documented model. Missing data remains unavailable.
  • Low/high launch targets retain their reference frequencies; approved square-root-frequency and linear-MHz models remain distinct.
  • TDR distance uses round-trip timing correctly and cannot cross an active device.
  • Power results are scalar RMS planning estimates, separate from mains power and measured quasi-square-wave behavior.
  • Records require consistent split and hardware/planning/live limits before entered measured values may be evaluated against limits. Planning-only rows cannot establish acceptance.
