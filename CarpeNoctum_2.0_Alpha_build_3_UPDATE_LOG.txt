2. Field calculators (build list item 4), Test -> Calculators
   - dB converter: dBmV, dBm, dBuV, mV, mW at 75 or 50 ohm.
   - Ripple to distance: 491.79 x VP / ripple spacing (published as 492).
   - Pre-EQ tap to distance: spacing between two mismatches (not the
     distance from the modem or node). DOCSIS 2.0/3.0 SC-QAM upstream,
     24 taps, 1.28 / 2.56 / 5.12 Msym/s, +/- half-tap range. Not OFDMA.
   - OFDM power: restate power per 6, 24 or 1.6 MHz and over the occupied
     band. Assumes flat power; a tilt needs SCTE 270 section 30.14.
   - Echo delay: round trip 2 x length / (VP x c).
   - VP from the four catalog cables that list one, or typed in (0.50-1.00).
   - Invalid or missing input shows a reason, never a number.

3. Tilt / EQ / pad solver (build list item 2), Test -> Calculators ->
