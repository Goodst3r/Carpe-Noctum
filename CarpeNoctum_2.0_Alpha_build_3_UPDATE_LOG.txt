CHANGES
1. Threshold and source registry (build list item 1)
   - Test -> Limits lists every limit, target and formula the field tools
     use, filterable by label: Spec, Expert, Company, Unknown.
   - Each entry keeps its source, revision, page or section, units,
     conditions, plant and checked date. Tapping a tag opens a source sheet.
   - Company values are read where they already live: the job's project
     profile, the meter setup TX references, or the built-in local preset
     (31 dBmV at 54 MHz, 43 dBmV at 860 MHz, separate 38 dBmV return TX).
     A blank value shows "Unknown - not entered"; an entered 0 stays 0.
   - Two existing approximations are labeled Unknown because no published
     source is recorded: the 0.1 %/F cable-loss temperature correction and
     the 0.004 per C default power-cable resistance coefficient.
   - No new storage. Opening the registry never changes the job.

2. Field calculators (build list item 4), Test -> Calculators
   - dB converter: dBmV, dBm, dBuV, mV, mW at 75 or 50 ohm.
   - Ripple to distance: 491.79 x VP / ripple spacing (published as 492).
   - Pre-EQ tap to distance: DOCSIS 2.0/3.0 SC-QAM upstream, 24 taps,
     1.28 / 2.56 / 5.12 Msym/s, +/- half-tap range. Not DOCSIS 3.1 OFDMA.
   - OFDM power: restate power per 6, 24 or 1.6 MHz and over the occupied
     band. Assumes flat power; a tilt needs SCTE 270 section 30.14.
   - Echo delay: round trip 2 x length / (VP x c).
   - VP from the four catalog cables that list one, or typed in (0.50-1.00).
   - Invalid or missing input shows a reason, never a number.

3. Practice library
   - Custom-curves exercise: two new guide steps (ten in total).
   - Feature index: entries 38 (calculators) and 39 (limits).

SOURCES CHECKED

reproduce every citation. See research/build3/SOURCE_VERIFICATION.md and
source-verification.json (36 findings: 33 primary, 1 secondary, 2 wrong
assumptions corrected). Key citations: SCTE 293-2 2024 section 4.1;
CableLabs CM-SP-PHYv3.0-I04 section 6.2.16 and CM-GL-PNMP-V03; CableLabs
CM-SP-PHYv3.1-I20 section 7.5.9.1; SCTE 258 2026; SCTE 280 2022 section 9.7;
Hranac, Communications Technology, Aug. 2005 and Nov. 2009. SCTE and
CableLabs text is paraphrased and cited by number only.
