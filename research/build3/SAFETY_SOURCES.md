# Pre-work safety card: where the wording came from (build list item 9)

Read November 11, 2026. Covers `night-sweep/safety.js` and the shared engine `night-sweep/checklist.js`.

## What the card is, and is not

A reminder list the technician ticks before climbing, opening plant or working near a road. It opens with
"Follow company policy" and says it is **not** any company's procedure. The wording is original: written for
this app from general field-safety practice. **No NCTI or SCTE course text, question or figure is copied.**

The list holds no number: no ladder angle, clearance distance, voltage, rating or time limit. Those belong to
a company's own rules, so the card says "as your company requires" and the technician can reword the list.
Because it has no limit, target, constant or formula, it adds **no row to the threshold registry**
(`night-sweep/thresholds.js`). The only things typed in are notes: a name or role of the person who knows
where the technician is, a check-in time, and where the company keeps its safety policy.

## Provenance label

Content: **expert**, general practice, **unverified**, not reviewed. The card states in its own footer that
"A supervisor or safety officer has not reviewed it yet."

## Open questions for a supervisor or safety officer

1. Does the company have a pre-work or job-safety-briefing form this card should match in section names or
   order? (The card must not be mistaken for it; it says it is not the procedure.)
2. Are there items a company would never want in a personal reminder list, or items that every technician must
   see and not be able to hide? (The card lets the technician hide, reword and add any original item.)
3. Is a "who knows where I am" role plus a check-in time what the company expects, or is there a required
   check-in system (radio, app, dispatcher call)?
4. May the device-local notes (a role and a time, never a customer detail) be kept on a personal phone?

## What this note does not claim

- No legal or regulatory requirement (OSHA, state or local rules, utility rules) is stated or implied.
- No NCTI or SCTE material beyond headings was read for this card; nothing was checked against a current
  standard; no safety standard or regulation was read.
